## Design Context

### Users
Sina and the RepeaterStore/Waveform team, testing home and office networks (Wi-Fi, CBRS radios) from phones on the local network, often side by side with the Ookla app. Several devices may test at once. Used standing up, phone in hand, glancing at the dial.

### Use cases
Run a test with one tap, watch it live, read three numbers (ping, download, upload) at a glance, and compare devices in the shared results list.

### Brand personality
A precise bench instrument that is a pleasure to watch: confident, calm, exact. The live readout should feel steady and trustworthy, never jumpy. Benchmark: Ookla's speedometer, but more refined.

### Aesthetic direction
"Test bench instrument": a graphite instrument face with engraved ticks and warm signal colors (amber for download, green for upload), condensed dashboard numerals (Saira), clean body text (Instrument Sans). Ticks and labels light up as the needle passes them. Light and dark themes both supported; dark is the instrument's home.

### Design principles
1. The dial is the hero; everything else is quiet.
2. Live numbers are the running result, so the final value is where the dial lands.
3. Results fill in the moment each stage finishes.
4. Color always paired with a label or icon.
5. Works one-handed at phone width; respects reduced motion.
